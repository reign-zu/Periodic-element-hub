Periodic Element Hub is a fast, lightweight web app for looking up the 118 chemical elements. Type an element’s name into the search bar to see its spectral image, a short summary, and details such as symbol, atomic number, category, phase, density, melting and boiling points, electronegativity and electron configuration.
